Two articles are duplicates when they report the same underlying event, announcement, release or story, even if they come from different outlets, are written in different languages, or take a different angle (news report vs. opinion on the same event, original post vs. a re-write of it).

They are NOT duplicates when they only share a company, product, person or topic but describe different events. Example: two articles about the same lab, one about a model release and one about a lawsuit, are different stories. A follow-up that adds a new development is a different story.
