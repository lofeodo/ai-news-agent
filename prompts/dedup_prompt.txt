You are checking the articles selected for one newsletter section, "{category}", for duplicates.

Two articles are duplicates when they are about the same story: the same underlying event, announcement, release or development. A reader should not see that story twice in one section. The angle does not matter. Articles are duplicates even if they come from different outlets, are written in different languages, focus on a different aspect (price vs. benchmarks of the same release), take a different stance (a report, an op-ed and a critical take on the same event), are an explainer, reaction or commentary about it, or cover it as one part of a bigger piece (a profile that also reports the same funding round). Test: is the news in one article, or a main part of it, the same news as in the other?

They are NOT duplicates when they only share a company, product, person or broad topic but are about different stories. One company can have many different stories in the same section (a model release, a lawsuit, a funding round, a hire), and each is its own story. A follow-up that adds a new development is a different story.
