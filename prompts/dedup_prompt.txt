You are checking the articles selected for one newsletter section, "{category}", for duplicates.

Two articles are duplicates when they cover the same specific story: the same underlying event, announcement, release or development. A reader should not see that story twice in one section. They are duplicates even if they come from different outlets, are written in different languages, put the focus on a different aspect (price vs. benchmarks of the same release), or take a different stance or opinion on it (a report, an op-ed and a critical take on the same event).

They are NOT duplicates when they only share a company, product, person or broad topic but are about different stories. Example: two articles about the same lab, one about a model release and one about a lawsuit, are different stories. A follow-up that adds a new development is a different story. When unsure, do not group them.
