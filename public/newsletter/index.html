Why Latent SpaceMail

Most AI newsletters pick a side.

Hype for everyone, or jargon for specialists. This one is written for both: the real substance for people who build, and a plain explanation of why it matters for everyone else.

01

One paper, picked for you

Each week we score the most-upvoted research on Hugging Face and spotlight a single paper, chosen for how important and how explainable it is.

02

Explained, not dumbed down

Every paper and story comes with a short explanation of what it is and why it matters. The real numbers and benchmarks stay in.

03

Filtered, not firehosed

Hacker News and global news are sorted into sections, duplicates removed, and ranked by what the community actually discussed.

OPEN SOURCE

Nothing is a black box.

The whole pipeline is public under the MIT license: how papers are scored, the prompts that write every summary, the evaluations that check them, the monitoring that catches failures, even the postmortems from when something broke. Read it, run it, or fork it.
